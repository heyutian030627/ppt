Level 1: 核心装备仓库 (Items & Equipment) Gear Up

⚔️ Weapons & Attack (武器类)
  • sword (剑) → use a sword
  • wand / magic staff (法杖) → cast a spell
  • bow & arrow (弓箭) → shoot the target
  • axe / hammer (战锤) → upgrade weapons
🛡️ Defense & Survival (防御生存)
  • shield (盾牌) → equip a shield
  • helmet / boots (头盔战靴) → wear a helmet
  • potion (药水/血瓶) → drink a potion
  • health kit (医药箱) → heal teammates
💎 Loot & Treasure (宝物道具)
  • key / master key (钥匙) → unlock doors
  • coin / gem (宝石) → collect shiny gems
  • map / compass (指南针) → follow the map
  • chest (宝箱) → open the secret chest

Level 2: 句型升级 (I like... because...) Sentence Power-Up

给出喜欢的理由，让表达更有说服力！

🧩 句型公式

I like [ 游戏/职业 ] because [ 他们/它 ... ].

🎮 常见例句
I like action games because they are exciting.
I like playing a Wizard because magic is creative.
💡 原因形容词词库 (Reason Bank)
  • ✨ exciting (令人兴奋的)
  • 🎨 creative (富有创造力的)
  • 🔥 difficult (有挑战性的)
  • ☕ relaxing (令人放松的)

Level 2: 语言积木双卡拼搭 (Power-Up Combos) Card Combos

抽取职业卡与装备卡，组合成完整表达！

🧩 Combo A: 职业 + 装备 (Class + Equipment)
I am a Wizard. I have a magic staff and a blue potion.
I am an Archer. I can shoot arrows with my bow.
🧩 Combo B: 任务 + 原因 (Quest + Reason)
I want to buy a golden shield because the dragon is very dangerous.
I usually team up with my pet to search for the secret treasure.

Level 3: 日常表达与能力转折 Daily & Skill Quests

⏰ 日常频率表达 (Frequency)
  • 🟢 always (总是)
  • 🔵 usually (通常)
  • 🟡 sometimes (有时候)
  • 🔴 never (从不)
I usually play games after school.
💪 能力与转折 (Can & But)

不要只说 Yes/No，加入 but 让表达更高级：

I can fight small enemies, but I can't beat the final boss.
I can use a sword, but I can't cast magic spells.

Level 3: 目标与未来计划 (Future Quest) I Want To...

使用 I want to... 阐述下一步游戏冒险目标！

🗺️ 探索目标
I want to explore the secret cave.
💎 收集目标
I want to collect 50 golden gems.
🐉 战斗目标
I want to defeat the fire dragon.
🌟 链式长句升级 (Chain Sentence)
I want to buy a golden shield because I want to beat the final boss.

👑 Level 4: 终极 Boss 决战 (The Boss Battle) 30s Final Speech

回答 5 个问题并串联成一段话，击败终极 Boss！

1. CLASS / GAME: I am an Explorer. I like adventure games...
2. REASON: ...because they are exciting and creative.
3. USUALLY: I usually play with my teammate after school.
4. CAN & BUT: I can collect shiny gems, but I can't beat the dragon.
5. WANT TO: I want to buy a golden shield next time!
